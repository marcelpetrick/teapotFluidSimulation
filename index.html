        How to read this

        Advection carries each parcel along the flow; colours are permanent labels, so a parcel never changes colour.

        Stirring rearranges parcels so different layers end up side by side. We measure it with a segregation score S computed in bins of 1, 2 and 4 cm: lower means more evenly mixed at that scale.

        Diffusion blurs properties at the molecular scale; it is negligible over seconds and is not what you see here.

        Removal is not mixing: pouring away one layer makes the rest look more uniform. The survivor-cohort comparison uses the same parcels before and after to separate the two.

        A pathline traces one parcel over time; it is not a streamline (the instantaneous flow direction).
